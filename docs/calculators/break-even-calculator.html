Break-Even Point Calculator

Determine the exact sales volume needed to cover your total fixed and variable costs.

How to Calculate Break-Even Point

The break-even point formula in units is:

Break-Even Units = Fixed Costs / (Price per Unit - Variable Cost per Unit)

Contribution margin per unit is the sales price minus variable cost per unit.
