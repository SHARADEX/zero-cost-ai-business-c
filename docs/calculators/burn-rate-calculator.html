How Startup Burn Rate & Runway Are Calculated

Gross Burn Rate: The total operating expenses incurred every month regardless of incoming revenue.

Net Burn Rate: Total Monthly Expenses - Total Monthly Revenue. This represents actual cash lost each month.

Runway (Months): Current Cash Balance / Net Monthly Burn. Indicates how long your company can operate before running out of funds.
