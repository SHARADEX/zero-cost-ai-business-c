How to Calculate Burn Rate and Runway

Your Gross Burn Rate is the total amount of cash spent on monthly operating expenses:

Gross Burn = Monthly Operating Expenses

Your Net Burn Rate is the actual monthly deficit when revenue is taken into account:

Net Burn = Monthly Operating Expenses - Monthly Revenue

Your Cash Runway tells you how many months your business can operate before running out of funds:

Runway (Months) = Cash Balance / Net Burn
