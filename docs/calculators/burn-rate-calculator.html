How to Calculate Burn Rate & Runway

Understanding your startup financial metrics is essential for planning fundraising rounds and sustaining growth.

  • Gross Burn Rate: Total monthly operating cash outlay (salaries, hosting, rent, SaaS subscriptions).
  • Net Burn Rate: Gross Burn Rate - Monthly Revenue. Represents real net cash loss each month.
  • Cash Runway: Current Cash Balance / Net Burn Rate (measured in months).
