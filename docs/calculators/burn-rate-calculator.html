How to Calculate Burn Rate and Cash Runway

Gross Burn Rate is the total amount of cash your business spends each month on operational expenses (salaries, rent, software subscriptions, marketing, server infrastructure).

Net Burn Rate is the actual net cash lost per month when accounting for incoming revenue:

Net Burn Rate = Total Monthly Expenses - Total Monthly Revenue

Cash Runway tells founders how many months the startup can operate before running out of funds:

Runway (Months) = Cash Balance / Net Burn Rate
