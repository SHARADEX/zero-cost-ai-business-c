What is Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)?

Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) is the predictable and recurring revenue components generated by customers over a 365-day period in a subscription or SaaS business model. It excludes one-off consulting fees, setup charges, and non-recurring hardware revenue.

The Standard ARR Formula

ARR = (Total Monthly Recurring Revenue) × 12

When computing Net New ARR over a fiscal cycle:

Net New ARR = New Bookings ARR + Expansion ARR - Churn/Contraction ARR

Why Track ARR?

  • Business Valuation: SaaS enterprise multiples are almost universally calculated as a multiple of forward-looking ARR.
  • Cash Runway Planning: Predict future cash flow to time hiring, infrastructure expansion, and product investments.
  • Churn Visibility: Uncovers whether sales growth is masking excessive churn and logo loss.
