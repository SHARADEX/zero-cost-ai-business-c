How MRR & ARR Are Calculated

Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) measures normalized monthly recurring subscription earnings. The standard formula for Net New MRR is:

Net New MRR = New MRR + Expansion MRR - Churned MRR

Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) is annualized regular income:

ARR = Ending MRR × 12
