How Customer Acquisition Cost is Calculated

The standard CAC formula divides total sales and marketing expenses by the number of new customers acquired during a specific time period:

CAC = (Total Marketing Spend + Total Sales Spend) / New Customers Acquired

Monitoring your CAC helps maintain unit economics and ensures your Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) is at least 3x your CAC (target LTV:CAC ratio is 3:1 or higher).
