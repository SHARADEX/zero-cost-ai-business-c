How Customer Lifetime Value Works

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV or LTV) measures total expected net revenue from a customer relationship. The standard formula used is:

CLV = Average Purchase Value × Purchase Frequency × Customer Lifespan × Gross Margin %

Knowing your CLV helps validate your Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC). Aim for an LTV:CAC ratio of 3:1 or higher for sustainable unit economics.
