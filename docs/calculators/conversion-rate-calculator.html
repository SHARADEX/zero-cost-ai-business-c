How is Conversion Rate Calculated?

The conversion rate formula is straightforward:

Conversion Rate (%) = (Total Conversions / Total Visitors) × 100

For example, if your landing page received 10,000 visitors and generated 350 newsletter signups, your conversion rate is (350 / 10,000) * 100 = 3.5%.
