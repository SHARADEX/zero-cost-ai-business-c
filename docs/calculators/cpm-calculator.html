How CPM is Calculated

CPM stands for Cost Per Mille (Mille = thousand in Latin). The formula is:

CPM = (Total Cost / Total Impressions) × 1,000

Use CPM to compare the cost efficiency of different ad networks, newsletter sponsorships, or display campaigns.
