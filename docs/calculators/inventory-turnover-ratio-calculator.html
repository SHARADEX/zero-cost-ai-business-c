Inventory Turnover Ratio Calculator

The inventory turnover ratio measures how many times a business sells and replaces its stock of goods over a period. A higher ratio typically indicates strong sales and efficient inventory management.

Formula

Inventory Turnover = Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) / Average Inventory

Where Average Inventory = (Beginning Inventory + Ending Inventory) / 2
