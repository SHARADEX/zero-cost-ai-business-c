What is Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)?

Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) represents the total cost associated with convincing a potential customer to buy a product or service. It includes all marketing spend, sales team salaries, and overhead divided by the number of customers gained during the measurement period.

Formula:

CAC = Total Sales & Marketing Expenses / Number of New Customers Acquired
