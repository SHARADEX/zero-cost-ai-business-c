What is Customer Lifetime Value?

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV or LTV) predicts the net profit contribution from the entire future relationship with a customer. Knowing your CLV helps determine how much you can profitably spend on acquiring new customers (CAC).

Formula

CLV = (ARPU × Gross Margin %) / Churn Rate
