How Startup Runway is Calculated

The standard formula for static runway is:

Runway (Months) = Total Cash Balance / Net Monthly Burn

Net Monthly Burn is calculated as total monthly expenses minus monthly revenue. If your revenue exceeds your expenses, your business is default alive and has infinite runway.
