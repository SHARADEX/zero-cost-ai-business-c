Understanding Startup Cash Runway

Startup runway is the number of months a company has before running out of cash, assuming current revenue and expenses stay constant. Calculating this metric accurately is crucial for timing fundraising, hiring decisions, and maintaining solvency.

Formula

Net Burn = Monthly Expenses - Monthly Revenue
Runway (Months) = Current Cash Balance / Net Burn

If revenue exceeds expenses, your company is cash-flow positive and theoretically has infinite runway.
