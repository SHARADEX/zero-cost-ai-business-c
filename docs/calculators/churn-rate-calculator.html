How Churn Rate Is Calculated

Customer churn rate represents the percentage of subscribers that cancel or do not renew during a given time period:

Customer Churn Rate = (Customers Lost in Period / Starting Customers) × 100

Similarly, Gross Revenue Churn measures the direct loss of Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) before factoring in expansion or new sales:

Revenue Churn Rate = (Lost MRR / Starting MRR) × 100

To calculate average customer lifetime in months, divide 1 by your monthly churn rate (e.g. 1 / 0.035 = 28.57 months).
