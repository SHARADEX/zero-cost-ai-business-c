What is EBITDA?

EBITDA stands for Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization. It is a widely used metric to evaluate a company's operating profitability by removing the impact of financing and accounting decisions.

Why Use EBITDA?

Investors and analysts use EBITDA to compare profitability between companies in the same industry, as it strips out non-operating variables.
