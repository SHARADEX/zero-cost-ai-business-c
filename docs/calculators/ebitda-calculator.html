What is EBITDA?

EBITDA stands for Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization. It is a widely used financial metric to assess a company's operational performance and cash-generation potential without distortion from financing decisions, tax jurisdictions, or non-cash accounting items.

EBITDA Formulas

  • Bottom-up: EBITDA = Net Income + Interest + Taxes + Depreciation + Amortization
  • Top-down: EBITDA = Operating Income (EBIT) + Depreciation + Amortization
  • EBITDA Margin: (EBITDA / Total Revenue) × 100
