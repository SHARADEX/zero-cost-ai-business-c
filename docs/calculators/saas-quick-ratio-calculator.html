The SaaS Quick Ratio measures your startup's ability to grow recurring revenue relative to churn. A quick ratio > 4.0 indicates stellar growth efficiency.

How SaaS Quick Ratio is Calculated

Formula: (New MRR + Expansion MRR) / (Churned MRR + Contraction MRR)

  • > 4.0: Excellent growth. Revenue additions significantly outpace churn.
  • 2.0 – 4.0: Moderate growth. Startup is growing, but churn requires optimization.
  • < 2.0: High churn risk. The business is running fast just to stay in place.
