How SaaS Quick Ratio is Calculated

The standard formula is:

Quick Ratio = (New MRR + Expansion MRR) / (Churned MRR + Contraction MRR)
  • < 1.0: Shrinking revenue base (Critical risk).
  • 1.0 - 2.0: Slow growth, high churn friction.
  • 2.0 - 4.0: Healthy growth trajectory.
  • > 4.0: Exceptional efficiency (Venture/hyper-growth standard).
