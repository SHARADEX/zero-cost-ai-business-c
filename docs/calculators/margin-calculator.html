Margin vs Markup: What is the Difference?

Margin (gross profit margin) is gross profit divided by total revenue. It represents the portion of sales revenue remaining after accounting for the cost of goods sold.

Markup is gross profit divided by cost. It is the percentage added to the cost price to determine the selling price.
