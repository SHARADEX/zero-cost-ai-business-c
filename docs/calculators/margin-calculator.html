How Profit Margin is Calculated

Gross profit margin is the percentage of revenue remaining after subtracting the cost of goods sold (COGS):

Margin (%) = ((Revenue - Cost) / Revenue) * 100

Markup is the percentage added onto the cost price to determine the selling price:

Markup (%) = ((Revenue - Cost) / Cost) * 100
