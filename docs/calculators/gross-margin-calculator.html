How to Calculate Gross Margin

The gross margin formula is:

Gross Margin (%) = ((Total Revenue - COGS) / Total Revenue) * 100

For example, if your store generated $50,000 in revenue with $20,000 in production costs, your gross profit is $30,000 and your gross margin is 60%.
