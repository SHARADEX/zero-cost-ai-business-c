Gross Margin Formula & Explanation

Gross Profit Margin measures the percentage of revenue remaining after subtracting direct production costs:

Gross Profit = Revenue - COGS
Gross Margin (%) = (Gross Profit / Revenue) * 100
Markup (%) = (Gross Profit / COGS) * 100

A higher gross margin indicates greater operational efficiency and more capital available to cover operating expenses.
