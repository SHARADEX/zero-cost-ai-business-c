Operating Margin Calculator

Operating margin measures how much profit a company makes on a dollar of sales after paying for variable costs of production, such as wages and raw materials, but before paying interest or tax.

Operating Margin: 0%

Operating Margin Formula

The standard formula for calculating operating profit margin is:

Operating Margin = (Operating Income / Total Revenue) * 100

A higher operating margin indicates that the company is more efficient at controlling operational costs relative to sales.
