Understanding LTV and CAC

Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) represents the total revenue a business can expect from a single customer over the course of their relationship. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) is the total cost of sales and marketing expenses incurred to acquire a new customer.

The LTV:CAC ratio measures unit economics. A ratio of 3:1 or higher indicates a sustainable SaaS business model where the value of a customer significantly exceeds the cost to acquire them. A ratio below 1:1 means you are losing money on every customer.
