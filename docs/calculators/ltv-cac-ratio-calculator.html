What is the LTV:CAC Ratio?

The LTV:CAC ratio compares the lifetime value of an average customer against the cost required to acquire that customer. It is a fundamental SaaS metric used by investors and founders to evaluate go-to-market efficiency.

How to interpret your score:

  • Less than 1.0: Losing money on every new customer acquired. Unsustainable.
  • 1.0 to 2.9: Low return on acquisition. Growth might be unprofitable.
  • 3.0 to 5.0: The SaaS ideal standard. Healthy growth and sustainable payback.
  • Greater than 5.0: Highly profitable, but you may be under-investing in acquisition and leaving growth on the table.
