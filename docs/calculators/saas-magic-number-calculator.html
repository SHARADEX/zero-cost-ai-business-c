How SaaS Magic Number Works

The SaaS Magic Number calculates sales efficiency by comparing annualized net new revenue against previous quarter sales and marketing expenditures:

Formula: (Current Quarter Revenue - Prior Quarter Revenue) × 4 / Prior Quarter S&M Expense

  • < 0.5: Low efficiency. Re-evaluate customer acquisition strategy before scaling spend.
  • 0.5 – 0.75: Moderate efficiency. Core engine working; refine unit economics.
  • 0.75 – 1.0+: High efficiency. Strong product-market fit; step on the gas and invest more in growth.
